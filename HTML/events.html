Past Events

Freshers' Night

The university hosted its annual Freshers' Night on October 9th, 2026, welcoming new students and celebrating their arrival. The event featured live music, performances, and a chance for students to connect with their peers and faculty members. Among the highlights were the talent showcases and interactive games that created a lively and engaging atmosphere for all attendees. The event was a great success, leaving lasting memories for the new students as they embarked on their academic journey at JKUAT.

Mr. and Miss. JKUAT

The university held its annual Mr. and Miss. JKUAT pageant on September 25th, 2026, showcasing the talents and charisma of our students. The winners were celebrated for their achievements and will represent the university in various events and activities throughout the year.
